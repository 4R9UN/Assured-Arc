# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback about code, documentation, safety, and developer experience.
- Protect private data and do not share credentials, customer information, or personal details.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private information, or hostile conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through the repository’s private security or maintainer contact channel when available. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors and users.
